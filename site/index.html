About the exam

Format
All multiple choice, 50 questions in 60 minutes, which is 72 seconds per question.
Question style
Mostly short scenarios like the in-class questions: a situation, a trace or a few lines of code, then five options.
How this site marks
Single-answer questions are all or nothing. Select-all questions have 2 to 4 correct options: each correct pick earns its share of the mark, but a single wrong pick scores the question 0.
Lectures
Introduction to networking, Internetworking, HTTP, DNS and email, Socket programming, Transport layer, UDP, Reliable data transfer, TCP.
Textbook
Kurose and Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach, 9th ed., Chapters 1 to 3.
Assignments
A1 (HTTP and DNS packet analysis) and A2 (UDP socket programming) appear in a few questions.
