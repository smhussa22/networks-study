Rules

  • The timer runs for 60 minutes and submits automatically at zero. That's 72 seconds per question.
  • Single-answer questions: full mark only for the one correct option.
  • Select-all questions have 2 to 4 correct options. Each correct pick earns its share of the mark, but one wrong pick scores the whole question 0. Blank scores 0.
  • Questions and options are shuffled for every attempt. Your order is saved, so a refresh keeps your place.
  • Answer in any order. Flag questions to come back to; the panel counts answered, unanswered and flagged questions.
